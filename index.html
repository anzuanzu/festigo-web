The world's great events, booked early

From Oktoberfest to the Sapporo Snow Festival, FestiGo follows 171 world-famous festivals, concerts, fashion shows and sporting events. It tells you when official dates are announced, when to book flights and hotels before prices climb, and links straight to official ticket channels. It also covers 67 bucket-list landmarks and 161 three-Michelin-star restaurants you can visit any time of year.

📱 FestiGo for iPhone is in development.
📅Verified dates

Dates are checked against official sources every week. If a date hasn't been announced yet, we show the usual timing instead of guessing.

⏰Book at the right time

Each event has a suggested booking window for flights and hotels, with a reminder when it opens.

🎟️Official tickets only

We never resell tickets. Ticket links always go to the organiser's official website.

🔔Change alerts

If a saved event moves dates or cities, FestiGo flags it and moves your reminders.
