🌺 Pasifika Festival

Auckland, New Zealand

Usually: A weekend in mid-March (2026: Mar 14–15)Dates TBA

The Southern Hemisphere's largest Pacific Islands festival: Samoan, Tongan, Fijian, Cook Islands and other villages with dance, music and island food—free entry.

Good to know

  • Every island village serves different food—come hungry.
  • It's at Western Springs; take the bus or shuttle—parking is scarce.
