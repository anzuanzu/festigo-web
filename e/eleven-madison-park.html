🌿 Eleven Madison Park

New York, United States

Daniel Humm · Contemporary American tasting menu (largely plant-based)

A three-star landmark overlooking Madison Square Park, led by chef Daniel Humm. The seasonal menu leans heavily on plants, with the option of a version that reintroduces animal proteins.

About US$385 per person for the full tasting menu; bar tasting menu about US$225, drinks extra

No dress code; many guests dress up, but wear whatever is most comfortable

Booking

Bookings are made online via Resy, released on the 1st of each month for the whole following month; if it's full, join the waitlist on Resy. Reservations are final and non-refundable.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • The full tasting menu runs about two to three hours and the bar menu about two, so set aside the whole evening.
  • Seats open on the 1st for the following month and prime slots go fast — book on Resy the moment it opens, or get on the waitlist early.
