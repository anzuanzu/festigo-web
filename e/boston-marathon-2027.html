🏃 Boston Marathon

Boston, United States

Apr 19, 2027

The world's oldest annual marathon and one of the seven World Marathon Majors, run on Patriots' Day from Hopkinton to Boylston Street.

Good to know

  • Runners need a qualifying time (BQ)—there's no open lottery. Spectating is free.
  • Wellesley's 'Scream Tunnel' and Heartbreak Hill are the classic spectator spots.
