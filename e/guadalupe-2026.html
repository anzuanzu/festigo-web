🌹 Feast of Our Lady of Guadalupe

Mexico City, Mexico

Dec 11 – 12, 2026

The world's most-visited Catholic shrine: around ten million pilgrims converge on the Basilica of Guadalupe, singing 'Las Mañanitas' to the Virgin at midnight on December 12.

Good to know

  • The midnight serenade from the 11th into the 12th is the most moving—and most crowded.
  • Take Metro Line 6 to La Villa-Basílica; nearby roads close.
