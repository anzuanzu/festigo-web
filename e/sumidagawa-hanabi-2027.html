🎆 Sumida River Fireworks

Asakusa, Tokyo, Japan

Usually: The last Saturday of July, usually announced in AprilDates TBA

Tokyo's signature summer fireworks—around 20,000 shells over the Sumida River beside Tokyo Skytree.

Good to know

  • Riverside viewing is free but packed—claim a spot by late afternoon, or book paid seats.
  • Some riverside restaurants and hotel rooms offer views—book early.
