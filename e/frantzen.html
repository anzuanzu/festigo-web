🍽️ Frantzén

Stockholm, Sweden

Björn Frantzén · Creative

Ring the bell, take an aperitif in the top-floor lounge while the day's ingredients are explained, then move to the counter and watch the kitchen at work. A seamless, deeply immersive three-star experience in central Stockholm.

SEK 5,500 per person, excluding beverages

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings and does not sell tables through third-party platforms, so you need to contact it directly; the documents give no fixed booking-opening date or window.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • It opens only Tuesday to Friday (Friday ends earlier), and is closed Saturday, Sunday and Monday, so plan your trip around those days.
  • It sits on Klara Norra Kyrkogata, a few minutes' walk from Stockholm Central Station and T-Centralen metro, so public transport is easiest.
