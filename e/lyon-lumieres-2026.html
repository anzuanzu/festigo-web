💡 Fête des Lumières

Lyon, France

Dec 5 – 8, 2026

For four nights around December 8, Lyon's churches, squares and riverbanks become a canvas for light art, drawing millions—rooted in the custom of placing candles in windows.

Good to know

  • Place des Terreaux and Fourvière are the main sites; weekends are packed.
  • Lyon hotels sell out early—consider staying nearby and taking the train in.
