★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026

🍷 Zilte

Antwerp, Belgium

Viki Geunes · Creative

Perched on the top floor of Antwerp's MAS museum with sweeping city views. Viki Geunes builds complex, gutsy creative dishes from superb ingredients without fussy frills.

Approximately €340 per person for the eight-course Pure Zilte tasting menu, excluding drinks

Casual chic – no shorts or flip-flops

Booking

Book online via the restaurant's own Tock page for up to 6 guests; for 6 or more, call +32 3 283 40 40 or email (phone lines open Mon–Fri 9:30 a.m.–8:00 p.m.).

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Open Monday to Friday only, with longer closures in September–November and around New Year – check the holiday periods before planning.
  • You enter via the MAS main entrance and take the lift to the 9th floor (escalators don't reach it); drivers should use Q-park Godefriduskaai.
