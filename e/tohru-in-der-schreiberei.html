🍣 Tohru in der Schreiberei

Munich, Germany

Tohru Nakamura · Contemporary Japanese-French, kaiseki-inspired

Set inside a 14th-century townhouse in Munich's old town, where Tohru Nakamura fuses French technique with a kaiseki sensibility. Only around 30 seats, and the evening menu runs about three and a half hours.

From about €360 per person for the tasting menu, drinks and pairings extra

Booking

New tables are released every Monday for three months ahead via the online booking system with credit-card verification; groups of more than six should email the restaurant.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Tables drop every Monday for three months ahead, so book the day they open; if two-person tables are gone, a four-person table may still be available. Cancelling within 48 hours or a no-show costs €450 per person.
  • The dining room sits on the first floor of a heritage building reached only by stairs (about 23 steps), so flag any mobility needs in advance. Last arrival is 8 pm and the full experience takes about 3.5 hours.
