🥢 Chao Shang Chao

Beijing, China

Yat Fung Cheung · Chaozhou (Teochew) cuisine

Chao Shang Chao (Chaoyang) is a three-Michelin-star destination for refined Chaozhou cooking, with opulent private rooms. Its braised goose and fish maw dishes showcase ingredient quality and precise technique.

About ¥1,500–3,000 per person

Formal

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings; reserve 4–6 weeks ahead, and private rooms require a deposit.
