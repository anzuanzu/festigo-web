🏙️ Petronas Twin Towers

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Best time: Year-round; May to July is a little drier

Twin 452-metre towers, once the world's tallest, clad in stainless steel shaped by Islamic geometry that glitters at night. A skybridge links them on the 41st floor.

Good to know

  • Observation deck tickets are limited; buy online in advance.
  • In the evening, KLCC Park below has a fountain show and the best night view.
