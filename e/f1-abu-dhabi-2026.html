🏁 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix

Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

Dec 4 – 6, 2026

The F1 season finale, starting at dusk and finishing under lights at Yas Marina, next to a superyacht harbour and Ferrari World.

Good to know

  • Race tickets often include the after-race concerts.
  • Yas Island hotels are closest, but Dubai is just over an hour away.
