🐎 Palio di Siena

Siena, Italy

Jul 2, 2027

A medieval horse race between Siena's districts: ten bareback horses race three laps of Piazza del Campo in about 90 seconds, after a grand historical pageant.

Good to know

  • Standing in the centre of the piazza is free, but you must enter by midday and stay until the end.
  • Races are on July 2 and August 16; trial runs take place in the three days before.
