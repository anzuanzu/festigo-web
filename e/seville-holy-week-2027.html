🕯️ Holy Week in Seville

Seville, Spain

Mar 21 – 28, 2027

Spain's most spectacular religious processions: in the week before Easter, 60-plus brotherhoods carry massive floats through the old town, accompanied by hooded penitents, brass bands and saetas.

Good to know

  • The night of Holy Thursday into Good Friday (La Madrugá) is the high point.
  • Buy Carrera Oficial seats if you want to watch sitting down.
