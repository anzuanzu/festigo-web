🥖 L'OSIER

Tokyo, Japan

Olivier Chaignon · French

A Ginza grande maison run by Shiseido, open since 1973 and long rated three Michelin stars. Chef Olivier Chaignon blends classical French technique with a modern sensibility.

About ¥20,000–65,000 per person plus 15% service charge, drinks extra

Jackets are required for gentlemen; no shorts, sandals or other casual clothing. No smoking inside.

Booking

Seats for three months ahead open simultaneously by phone and online at 10 AM on the first business day of each month; phone lines run 10:00–20:00, and credit card details are required in advance for every booking, including phone reservations.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Seats for three months out open at 10 AM Japan time on the first business day of each month and often go fast — have your credit card ready and try phone and web at the same time.
  • It sits in Ginza 7-chome, a few minutes' walk from Ginza Station; allow about two hours for lunch and three for dinner, and note that guests must be 13 or older.
