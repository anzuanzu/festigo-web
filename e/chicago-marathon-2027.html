🏃 Chicago Marathon

Chicago, United States

Oct 10, 2027

One of the seven Majors: a flat loop through 29 Chicago neighborhoods, starting and finishing in Grant Park before over a million spectators.

Good to know

  • Entry is by lottery (apply in October, results in December the year before). Spectating is free.
  • October mornings in Chicago are chilly—bring an extra layer.
