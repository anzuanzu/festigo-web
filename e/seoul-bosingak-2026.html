🔔 Seoul Bosingak New Year Bell Ringing

Seoul, South Korea

Dec 31, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027

At midnight the Bosingak pavilion in Jongno rings its bell 33 times—Korea's signature New Year ritual, broadcast nationwide.

Good to know

  • Jongno gets packed and freezing—dress warmly.
  • Seoul's subway runs extended hours on the night.
