🦞 Maine Lobster Festival

Rockland, United States

Aug 4 – 8, 2027

New England's most famous seafood festival—tens of thousands of pounds of steamed lobster over five days, plus a parade.

Good to know

  • Lobster dinners are sold on site—no need to pre-book.
  • Drive Route 1 to see Maine's coastal lighthouses.
