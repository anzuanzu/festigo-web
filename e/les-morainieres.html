🍷 Les Morainières

Jongieux, France

Michaël Arnoult · Creative, Modern French

A three-star modern French restaurant on the vineyard slopes of Savoie, where Michaël Arnoult cooks with local producers and the seasons. Classical precision, quiet rather than theatrical, with a jellied crayfish tartare as its signature.

About €220–300 per person for the tasting menu, drinks extra

Formal

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings, so you must contact it directly; plan six to eight weeks ahead, especially for weekend lunch.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Open Thursday to Sunday only; target the weekend lunch, when the long service leaves you time to explore the wine country afterwards.
  • Booking is close to impossible at short notice — reserve directly with the restaurant at least six to eight weeks out, earlier in high season.
