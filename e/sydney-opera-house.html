🎭 Sydney Opera House

Sydney, Australia

Best time: September–November and March–May, warm without the summer heat

The sail-shaped Opera House and the 'Coathanger' Harbour Bridge make Sydney Harbour's classic view. Catch a show inside or climb the bridge arch.

Good to know

  • The one-hour tour is good, but seeing a performance is better.
  • Take the ferry to Manly for views of both from the water.
