🍓 Dahu Strawberry Festival

Dahu, Miaoli, Taiwan

Usually: Strawberry season December to April, peaking January to MarchDates TBA

Taiwan's strawberry capital, where hillsides of pick-your-own farms, a strawberry winery and museum open from winter to spring.

Good to know

  • January to March is peak season—go on a weekday to avoid queues.
  • Combine it with the Tai'an hot springs nearby.
