💦 Songkran Water Festival

Bangkok, Thailand

Apr 13 – 15, 2027

Thai New Year turns the whole country into a giant water fight. Bangkok's Silom and Khao San Road are the hottest zones.

Good to know

  • Bring a waterproof phone pouch.
  • Domestic flights and buses are also busy over the holiday.
