🎡 Cannstatter Volksfest

Stuttgart, Germany

Sep 24 – Oct 10, 2027

Germany's second-largest beer festival after Oktoberfest—fewer tourists, friendlier prices and a more local crowd.

Good to know

  • Combine it with Oktoberfest—the cities are two hours apart by train.
  • Tent reservations are far easier than in Munich.
