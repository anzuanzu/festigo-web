🕋 Hajj (Muslims only)

Mecca, Saudi Arabia

Usually: 8–13 Dhul Hijjah, confirmed by moon sighting; Arafat Day 2027 is expected on May 15Dates TBA

One of the Five Pillars of Islam and the world's largest annual pilgrimage: about two million Muslims perform the rites in Mecca, Mina and Arafat, ending with Eid al-Adha.

Muslims only. Pilgrims need a Hajj visa issued by Saudi Arabia, arranged through their country's quota and an approved operator; non-Muslims may not enter Mecca.

Good to know

  • Register through your country's Hajj quota and a Saudi-approved operator—places are limited.
  • Mecca in May often exceeds 40°C—stay hydrated and shaded.
