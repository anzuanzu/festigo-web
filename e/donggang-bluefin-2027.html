🐟 Pingtung Bluefin Tuna Festival

Donggang, Pingtung, Taiwan

Usually: Early May to early JulyDates TBA

Each May, bluefin tuna migrate past southern Taiwan; Donggang's first-catch auction makes headlines and fresh-cut sashimi is the highlight.

Good to know

  • Huaqiao Market is freshest in the morning and cheaper than restaurants.
  • Ferries to Xiaoliuqiu leave from Donggang.
