☀️ Summer Sonic

Makuhari, Chiba, Japan

Usually: A weekend in mid-AugustDates TBA

Japan's biggest city music festival, held simultaneously in Tokyo (Makuhari) and Osaka with global headliners and Japanese acts.

Good to know

  • Tokyo and Osaka share the lineup on swapped days—pick one city.
  • August is hot; the site mixes indoor and outdoor stages—stay hydrated.
