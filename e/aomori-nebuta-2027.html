🏮 Aomori Nebuta Matsuri

Aomori, Japan

Aug 2 – 7, 2027

Enormous illuminated warrior floats parade at night, and anyone in the official costume can join the haneto dancers.

Good to know

  • Rent a haneto costume to dance in the parade.
  • Aomori has few hotels—consider Hirosaki or Hachinohe.
