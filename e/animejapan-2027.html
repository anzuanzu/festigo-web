🎬 AnimeJapan

Osaka, Japan

Mar 27 – 28, 2027

One of the world's largest anime expos, held outside Tokyo for the first time in 2027, with studio premieres and exclusive merchandise.

Good to know

  • Public days are the weekend of March 27–28; the weekdays are for industry.
  • The venue is Intex Osaka in Nanko; stay in central Osaka and take the subway.
