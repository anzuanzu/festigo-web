🍽️ Les Prés d'Eugénie - Michel Guérard

Eugénie-les-Bains, France

Hugo Souchet · French fine dining

A three-star legend since 1977, now led by Hugo Souchet, carrying on Michel Guérard's naturalist French cuisine. Historic salons and gardens make it a destination for both dining and the estate experience.

From about €155 per person, drinks extra
