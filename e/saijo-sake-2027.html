🍶 Saijo Sake Matsuri

Saijo, Higashihiroshima, Japan

Usually: The second weekend of October (Sports Day holiday)Dates TBA

One of Japan's three great sake towns, where you can taste around a thousand sakes from across Japan while seven local breweries open their doors.

Good to know

  • The Sake Hiroba ticket is all-you-can-taste—drink water between sips.
  • It's a 35-minute JR ride from Hiroshima City.
