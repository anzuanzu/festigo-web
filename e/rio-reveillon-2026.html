🎆 Rio New Year's Eve at Copacabana

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Dec 31, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027

Over two million people dressed in white pack Copacabana Beach for offshore fireworks and beach-stage concerts—one of the largest New Year's parties on Earth.

Good to know

  • Wear white, and jump seven waves after midnight for luck.
  • Crowds are enormous—carry only essentials and watch for pickpockets; the metro usually needs a pre-bought special card that night.
