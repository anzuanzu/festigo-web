Hiroshi Sasaki · Japanese kaiseki

Hiroshi Sasaki and his apprentices cook right in front of you, giving classic Kyoto kaiseki a light, modern touch. It is ingredient-driven and full of stage presence — a theatrical take on kaiseki.

About ¥22,000 for lunch and ¥44,000 for dinner, tax and service included, drinks extra
