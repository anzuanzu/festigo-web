🚞 Jungfraujoch

Interlaken, Switzerland

Best time: June to September for clear days; winter for skiing

A cog railway tunnels through the Eiger to the Top of Europe at 3,454 metres, facing the Aletsch Glacier and Alpine peaks. Grindelwald and Lauterbrunnen below look like postcards.

Good to know

  • Check the summit webcam before going and pick a clear day.
  • Swiss Travel Pass and Jungfrau regional passes give discounts.
