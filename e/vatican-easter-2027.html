Vatican City, Vatican City

Mar 25 – 28, 2027

Catholicism's most important feast: Holy Week culminates in the Easter Mass on a flower-filled St Peter's Square and the Pope's Urbi et Orbi blessing.

Good to know

  • Easter Mass tickets are free on request; standing room in the square needs none.
