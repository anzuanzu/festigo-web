★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026 (since 2025)

🍣 Sushi Sho

New York, United States

Keiji Nakazawa · Edomae-style omakase sushi

Keiji Nakazawa's Edomae sushi counter near Bryant Park earned a third Michelin star in 2025, the first New York sushi restaurant to do so since Masa. Just ten seats, with an omakase followed by a choose-your-own okonomi round.

US$450 per person for the omakase (tax, drinks and okonomi extra); okonomi pieces run US$25–50 each, budget around US$800 with sake

Booking

Bookings are online only and open two months ahead, with new seats released on the first of each month at 11am; you must confirm your reservation by text, phone or email the day before.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • With only ten seats and two seatings a night, tables go fast: to eat in a given month, watch the booking page at 11am on the first day of the month two months prior.
  • The roughly 20-course omakase is US$450, but the okonomi add-ons are the highlight—plan on at least five more pieces, plus the US$180 eight-sake pairing; no tipping.
