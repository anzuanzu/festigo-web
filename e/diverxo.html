🐷 DiverXO

Madrid, Spain

Dabiz Muñoz · Creative

Dabiz Muñoz’s DiverXO is known for its single “Flying Pigs” tasting menu, blending Spanish ingredients with Asian influences in a playful, avant-garde experience. It is a three-Michelin-star destination in Madrid.

About €450 per person (tax included), drinks extra

No dress code; come as you wish

Booking

Bookings open daily at 00:00 for a new date 90 days ahead; a €450 per-person ticket is required and deducted from the final bill.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Booking is highly competitive: a new date 90 days ahead opens daily at midnight, so be ready to book right on time.
  • Open Tuesday to Friday for lunch and dinner; the nearest Metro is Cuzco on Line 10, about a six-minute walk. There is no dress code.
