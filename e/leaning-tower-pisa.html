🔔 Leaning Tower of Pisa

Pisa, Italy

Best time: April–June and September–October

The marble bell tower leaning nearly four degrees on the Field of Miracles, beside the cathedral and baptistery. Climb its 251 spiral steps and you feel the tilt underfoot.

Good to know

  • Climbing needs a timed booking, and children under 8 aren't allowed.
  • It is about an hour by train from Florence, an easy day trip.
