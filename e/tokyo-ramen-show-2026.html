🍜 Tokyo Ramen Festa

Tokyo, Japan

Oct 30 – Nov 3, 2026

Famous ramen shops from Hokkaido to Kyushu gather at Komazawa Olympic Park for five days. Renamed Tokyo Ramen Festa from 2026.

Good to know

  • Buy meal tickets at the entrance; bowls are smaller so you can try several.
  • Advance tickets close the day before opening and are cheaper than at the gate.
