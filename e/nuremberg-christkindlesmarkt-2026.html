🎄 Nuremberg Christkindlesmarkt

Nuremberg, Germany

Nov 27 – Dec 24, 2026

One of Germany's most famous Christmas markets, dating back to the 17th century. Mulled wine, Lebkuchen and Nuremberg sausages are must-tries.

Good to know

  • Weekends are packed; weekday evenings are best.
  • Pair it with a German rail pass to visit other markets.
