🥖 Cheval Blanc by Peter Knogl

Basel, Switzerland

Peter Knogl · French haute cuisine with Mediterranean and Asian influences

A flagship restaurant in Basel’s historic Grand Hotel Les Trois Rois, known for precise French haute cuisine. Chef Peter Knogl combines flawless technique with exceptional ingredients for a refined, memorable meal.

CHF 210 for the 4-course lunch with coffee; dinner tasting menu price not stated

Elegant attire; no sneakers, jogging clothes or sandals, and men should wear a long-sleeved shirt and long trousers. Dogs are not allowed, and phone calls should be avoided in the restaurant.

Booking

The restaurant manages its own bookings; contact it directly by phone at +41 61 260 50 07 or by email at chevalblanc@lestroisrois.com. No advance booking window is stated.
