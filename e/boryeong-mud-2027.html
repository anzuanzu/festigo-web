🟤 Boryeong Mud Festival

Boryeong, South Korea

Usually: About two and a half weeks from late July to early AugustDates TBA

Mud wrestling, mud slides and mud fights on Daecheon Beach—Korea's most popular summer festival with foreign visitors.

Good to know

  • Wear old clothes and bring a waterproof phone pouch.
  • Day tours from Seoul include direct shuttle buses.
