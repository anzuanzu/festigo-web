🐍 Chichén Itzá

Yucatán, Mexico

Best time: November to March, cool and dry; at the equinoxes the serpent shadow descends the pyramid

The greatest city of the Maya. El Castillo's 365 steps mark the days of the year, and at the equinoxes sunlight casts a serpent of shadow down its stairs. One of the New Seven Wonders.

Good to know

  • Arrive at the 8 am opening, ahead of the Cancún tour buses and the midday sun.
  • Cool off with a swim in the nearby Ik Kil cenote.
