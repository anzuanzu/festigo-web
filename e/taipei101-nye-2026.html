🎇 Taipei 101 New Year's Eve Fireworks

Xinyi, Taipei, Taiwan

Dec 31, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027

At midnight, the whole Taipei 101 tower erupts in fireworks—one of the world's most-watched New Year shows, with a big concert in Xinyi.

Good to know

  • Elephant Mountain and Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall are top viewpoints—claim a spot by afternoon.
  • Xinyi hotels are pricey and sell out early; stay elsewhere along the MRT.
