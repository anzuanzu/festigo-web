Jordi Cruz · Creative

Jordi Cruz's creative fine-dining restaurant blends seasonal ingredients with Catalan and Mediterranean flavours. There is a single tasting menu, starting with appetisers in the kitchen, and the Aqua Mirabilis dessert is a signature.

About €295–315 per person, drinks extra

Booking

Book online 24/7; a €100 per-person deposit is required and deducted from the final bill. Changes or cancellations are allowed up to 48 hours ahead for 1–9 guests, and 10 days ahead for 10 or more.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Only a tasting menu is offered, with no à la carte; there is no dedicated vegetarian menu, so state allergies or intolerances when booking.
  • The meal lasts at least three hours, so allow the full time slot; drivers can use the free restaurant car park, with the entrance on Avenida del Tibidabo.
