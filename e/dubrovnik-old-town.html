⚓ Dubrovnik Old Town

Dubrovnik, Croatia

Best time: May–June and September–October, avoiding peak cruise-ship crowds

The Pearl of the Adriatic: terracotta roofs inside intact medieval walls, and King's Landing in Game of Thrones.

Good to know

  • Walking the walls takes about two hours; go at the 8 am opening before the heat.
  • The cable car up Mount Srđ looks down over the old town and the sea.
