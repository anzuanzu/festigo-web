🧨 Yanshui Beehive Fireworks

Yanshui, Tainan, Taiwan

Feb 19 – 20, 2027

Taiwan's wildest Lantern Festival tradition: thousands of bottle rockets fire from 'beehive' towers into the crowd, believed to bring good luck.

Good to know

  • Full protective gear is a must: full-face helmet, thick jacket, long pants and gloves.
  • Watch from the edges or a netted viewing area if you prefer.
