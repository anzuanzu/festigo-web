🥖 Les Amis

Singapore, Singapore

Sébastien Lepinoy · Modern French

A pioneer of Singapore's fine-dining scene, where chef Sébastien Lepinoy builds modern French classics on produce flown in from France. Velvet walls and crystal chandeliers make it a favourite for dates and celebrations.

About S$220 per person and up for dinner; lunch sets from S$108, drinks extra

Smart casual: gentlemen in long trousers and covered shoes, ladies chic and polished

Booking

Book online via Chope with a credit card to secure the table; parties of five or more should email lesamis@lesamis.com.sg. The 3-course prix fixe lunch runs Monday to Thursday only, excluding public holidays.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Book through Chope with a credit card, and email for parties of five or more; weekend dinner slots go first, so plan ahead.
  • It is about a one-minute walk from Orchard MRT; the restaurant only welcomes guests aged 10 and above, and because the menu uses dairy and eggs, dietary requests can only be partly accommodated.
