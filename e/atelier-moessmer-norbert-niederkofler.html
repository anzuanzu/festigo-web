🏔️ Atelier Moessmer Norbert Niederkofler

Brunico, Italy

Norbert Niederkofler · Creative, Alpine

Set in a historic villa and park in Brunico, it serves a single seasonal tasting menu built on the chef's 'Cook the Mountain' philosophy. The cooking is tightly bound to the local terrain and ranks among Italy's contemporary benchmarks.

About €320 per person for the single 'Cook the Mountain' tasting menu, drinks extra

Booking

Book online for up to 6 guests; larger groups must email reservations@ateliernorbertniederkofler.com. The table is only confirmed once credit-card details are provided, and cancellation is free up to 72 hours before.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • With only 30 seats and one menu, weekend lunch and dinner slots go fastest — book online as early as you can.
  • Wednesday to Friday are dinner only, Saturday serves lunch and dinner, Sunday lunch only, and it is closed Monday and Tuesday.
