🍺 Oktoberfest

Munich, Germany

Sep 18 – Oct 3, 2027

The world's largest beer festival, drawing around six million visitors. Traditional dress, giant beer tents and one-litre steins are its trademarks.

Good to know

  • Tent reservations open early in the year and popular tents fill fast.
  • Opening weekend and German Unity Day are the priciest nights.
