💧 Timkat (Ethiopian Epiphany)

Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Jan 18 – 20, 2027

A UNESCO-listed Ethiopian Orthodox celebration of Christ's baptism: on the eve, replicas of the Ark (tabots) are carried to the water amid white-robed crowds; at dawn the water is blessed and sprinkled over everyone.

Good to know

  • The most famous celebrations are at Fasilides' Bath in Gondar and in Lalibela, both in the Amhara region—check your government's travel advisory first.
  • In Addis Ababa, join the celebrations at Jan Meda; white or light clothing fits right in.
