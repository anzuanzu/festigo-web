🏁 Indianapolis 500

Indianapolis, United States

May 30, 2027

The world's largest single-day sporting event: over 300,000 fans watch 33 cars race 500 miles at 230 mph, and the winner drinks milk—one leg of motorsport's Triple Crown.

Good to know

  • The 500 Festival Parade runs downtown the day before—plan to see both.
  • The infield is the party; grandstands are better for watching the race.
