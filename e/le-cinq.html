🥖 Le Cinq

Paris, France

Christian Le Squer · Modern French

A three-star dining room inside the Four Seasons George V, all gilded columns, garden light and palace formality. Le Squer builds his classical French cooking around impeccable seafood and precise sauces, with desserts and service to match.

About €620–€640 per person for the evening tasting menu, excluding drinks (lunch: €175 for four courses, €225 for six courses)

Jacket required

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings, so reserve directly with Le Cinq or through the Four Seasons website; plan ahead for peak dates.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Dinner only, Tuesday to Saturday 19:00–21:30; closed Sunday and Monday, and lunch service has been discontinued.
  • Book six to eight weeks ahead for a weekend dinner, and remember a jacket is required.
