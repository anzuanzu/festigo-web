Sonoma, United States

Brian Limoges · Modern California coastal cuisine with French technique

A 30-seat Victorian house where chef Brian Limoges tells Sonoma's seasonal story through an eight-to-ten course menu. French technique meets New England memories and California coastal flavors, much of it grown on a sustainable farm.

About US$250 per person, plus a 20% service charge and US$5 fee; drinks extra

No shorts, sandals, athleisure wear or hats

Booking

Bookings are released only via Tock on the last Friday of each month at 12:00 PM PST, two months ahead; a waitlist is also available.
