🍷 Pic

Valence, France

Anne-Sophie Pic · Creative French

A legendary family house in Valence run by Anne-Sophie Pic, shaped by her love of Japan and intricate sauces. There is a single set menu of abstractly named "ports of call" whose dishes are revealed only when they arrive.

From about €390 per person; the 7-course dinner with 8 wine pairings is €650 including tax

Booking

The house handles its own bookings: use the online booking page, call +33 4 75 44 15 32, or email contact@pic-valence.com. The Michelin Guide likewise advises contacting the restaurant directly to reserve.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • There is only one menu: seven courses at lunch and ten at dinner, so choose your slot accordingly if you want the full experience.
  • Closed Sunday and Monday, and seating windows are narrow (lunch 12:00–13:00, dinner 19:00–20:15), so arrive on time.
