★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026

🦀 Aponiente

El Puerto de Santa María, Spain

Ángel León · Creative seafood cuisine

Housed in a 19th-century tide mill, this kitchen turns plankton, seawater and discarded fish into avant-garde cooking. Before the meal, guests cross wooden walkways through restored marshland.

About €750 per person for the tasting menu; documents also list inconsistent or older prices such as €290, €215, and €160/€190

No formal dress code, but long trousers and closed shoes are recommended

Booking

Bookings and changes go through the reservations team by phone (+34 606 22 58 59) or email (reservas@aponiente.com), subject to availability.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • The tasting menu runs about 3–3.5 hours; arrive on time or part of the menu may be cut.
  • Cancel or reduce the party at least 72 hours ahead (5 days for 7+ guests), or the card given at booking is charged.
