💃 Seville April Fair

Seville, Spain

Apr 13 – 18, 2027

A temporary city of a thousand striped tents where people dance sevillanas in flamenco dress, ride horses and drink sherry until dawn.

Good to know

  • Most casetas are private—look for the public ones.
  • Hotel prices often double or triple during the fair.
