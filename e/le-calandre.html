🍝 Le Calandre

Rubano, Italy

Massimiliano (Max) Alajmo · Creative, contemporary Italian

The Alajmo family's three-star flagship, where Max Alajmo builds playful contemporary cooking on Italian classics. Its three tasting menus can be mixed freely, course by course, making it a destination worth the detour from Venice.

Tasting menus from about €280 per person, excluding drinks

Booking

Book online through SevenRooms on alajmo.it, ideally several weeks ahead; same-day requests are phone-only (+39 049 630303), and parties of more than six must arrange directly with the restaurant.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Book online several weeks ahead; for same-day or last-minute tables you have to call, and groups over six should contact the restaurant directly.
  • Closed Monday and Sunday; lunch and dinner run Wednesday to Saturday, while Tuesday is dinner only — plan accordingly.
