🌿 Te Matatini National Kapa Haka Festival

Hopuhopu, Waikato, New Zealand

Feb 22 – 27, 2027

New Zealand's biennial national kapa haka championship—dozens of Māori teams compete in haka, song and dance at one of the world's largest Indigenous performing-arts gatherings.

Good to know

  • Finals-day tickets are the hardest to get—buy early.
  • The venue is north of Hamilton; stay there and drive or take the shuttle.
