🍷 Steirereck im Stadtpark

Vienna, Austria

Heinz Reitbauer (with Michael Bauböck) · Creative, Modern Austrian

A glass pavilion in Vienna’s Stadtpark serving precise, modern Austrian cooking. The bread and cheese trolleys and polished service are part of the experience.

Dinner tasting menu €225 (six courses) or €245 (seven courses) per person, excluding drinks; lunch €155 to €175

Booking

Reservations are handled directly by the restaurant; for more than 6 guests, contact the reservations team Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Book directly with the restaurant; parties over 6 need to call ahead, and popular times are best arranged early.
  • Take U4 to Stadtpark or U3 to Stubentor/Wien Mitte and walk; lunch offers a flexible 4- or 5-course option.
