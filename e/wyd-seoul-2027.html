🕊️ World Youth Day Seoul 2027

Seoul, South Korea

Aug 3 – 8, 2027

The global Catholic youth gathering, held every two to three years with a closing Mass led by the Pope—over a million expected, in Korea for the first time.

Good to know

  • Pilgrims register through their diocese or the official site, which includes lodging and meals.
  • The closing Mass draws huge crowds; Seoul hotels fill early, so consider Gyeonggi.
