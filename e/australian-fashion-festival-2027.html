👠 Australian Fashion Festival

Melbourne, Australia

Feb 19 – Mar 6, 2027

Formerly the Melbourne Fashion Festival, this 30-year-old public festival stages its main runways at the Royal Exhibition Building, with most shows open to ticket buyers.

Good to know

  • Beyond the main runways, the city hosts many free exhibitions and brand events.
  • Pair it with the Melbourne Food & Wine Festival in March.
