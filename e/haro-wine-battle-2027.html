🍷 Haro Wine Battle

Haro, Spain

Jun 29, 2027

A Rioja summer tradition where thousands in white climb a hill and drench each other in red wine with buckets and squirt guns.

Good to know

  • Wear white with a red scarf and waterproof your phone.
  • Afterwards, tour the historic wineries in the Barrio de la Estación.
