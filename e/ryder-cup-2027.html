⛳ Ryder Cup

Adare, Limerick, Ireland

Sep 17 – 19, 2027

Golf's Europe-versus-USA team contest, held every two years. The 2027 edition marks its centenary and returns to Ireland, at Adare Manor.

Good to know

  • Tickets are allocated by registration and ballot in 2026—register on the official site.
  • Practice days (Sep 13–16) are easier to get into and let you see players up close.
