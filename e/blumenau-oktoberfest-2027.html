🍺 Oktoberfest Blumenau

Blumenau, Brazil

Usually: Opens on a Wednesday in early October and runs about 19 days (2026: Oct 7–25)Dates TBA

The Americas' largest German festival: nearly three weeks of parades, traditional dress, beer and German food in the German-settled city of Blumenau.

Good to know

  • Downtown parades run on Wednesdays and Saturdays—check the schedule.
  • Fly via São Paulo to Navegantes airport.
