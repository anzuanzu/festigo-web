🏹 Naadam Festival

Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia

Jul 11 – 15, 2027

Mongolia's national festival of the 'three manly games'—wrestling, horse racing and archery—with a grand opening ceremony and child jockeys racing across the steppe.

Good to know

  • Buy opening-ceremony tickets (Jul 11) early—tour operators snap them up.
  • Horse races are held on the steppe outside the city—join a tour or hire a driver.
