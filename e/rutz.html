★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026 (since 2020)

🍷 Rutz

Berlin, Germany

Marco Müller · Modern Cuisine, Creative

Marco Müller's 12- to 13-course 'Inspiration' menu builds a clear narrative arc, executed with precision yet a sense of effortlessness. Produce comes from small suppliers he knows personally, and there is a small terrace for summer.

About €340–360 per person for the 12- or 13-'inspiration' menu, drinks extra; wine pairing from €179 for 7 glasses

Smart/formal attire recommended

Booking

Reservations only, via the website or by phone at +49 30 24628760; book several weeks ahead, and allow about three hours for the menu.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Dinner only and closed on Saturdays and Sundays; with very few seats, book as soon as reservations open.
  • Ask for a terrace table in summer; the wine list and non-alcoholic pairings are both renowned, so let the sommelier guide you.
