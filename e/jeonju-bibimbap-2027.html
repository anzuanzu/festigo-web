🍚 Jeonju Bibimbap Festival

Jeonju, South Korea

Usually: October; held at Deokjin Park since 2026Dates TBA

Bibimbap's hometown holds its food festival in the Hanok Village, famous for a giant bibimbap mixed by hundreds of people.

Good to know

  • Rent a hanbok to explore the Hanok Village—many shops offer discounts.
  • About 90 minutes from Seoul by KTX.
