🐂 San Fermín

Pamplona, Spain

Jul 6 – 14, 2027

Every morning at 8, bulls charge through the old town to the bullring—made world-famous by Hemingway's The Sun Also Rises.

Good to know

  • Rent a balcony along the route for a safe, clear view.
  • Everyone wears white with a red neckerchief.
