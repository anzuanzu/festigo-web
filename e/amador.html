🍷 Amador

Vienna, Austria

Juan Amador · Creative, Modern

Set inside a Grinzing wine estate, the dining room pairs brick vaulting with views into a glass-walled barrel cellar. Juan Amador's cooking turns top ingredients into a precise, refined modern tasting menu.

Approximately €395 per person for the tasting menu, excluding drinks

No dress code; smart-casual attire is recommended, but no tie required

Booking

Book through the online reservation system on the restaurant's website; phone bookings are handled Wednesday to Friday, 13:00–17:00. Credit card details must be supplied within 48 hours or the booking may be cancelled.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Open Wednesday to Saturday only, with a Saturday lunch service as well; for prime dates, book eight to twelve weeks ahead.
  • The restaurant sits in Grinzing on the city's northern edge, about a fifteen-minute taxi ride from the centre — arrange your ride back in advance.
