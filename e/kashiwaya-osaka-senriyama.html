🥢 Kashiwaya Osaka Senriyama

Osaka, Japan

Hideaki Matsuo · Japanese kaiseki (ryotei)

A traditional ryotei in Suita, Osaka, where chef Hideaki Matsuo builds seasonal kaiseki menus around Naniwa heirloom vegetables. Quiet private rooms suit special occasions.

About ¥17,600–66,000 per person (tax included), plus a 10% service charge

Booking

Book online through OMAKASE (opens February 1) or by phone at 06-6386-2234 (from February 3). Reservations open on the 1st of the month, three months before your visit date.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Reservations only, bookable online via OMAKASE; the meal runs about three hours, so allow plenty of time and tell them in advance about any return flight.
  • There are stairs inside, and vegetarian or gluten-free menus cannot be arranged; on regular days guests must be 12 or older. The nearest station is Kandaimae.
