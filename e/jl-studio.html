★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026 (since 2023)

🦀 JL Studio

Taichung, Taiwan

Jimmy Lim Tyan Yaw · Modern Singaporean

Singaporean chef Jimmy Lim reworks the flavours of his homeland through Taiwanese produce at this Taichung dining room. Satay and chilli crab are broken down and rebuilt into a refined, quietly playful multi-course menu.

Tasting menu from about US$145 per person, drinks extra

Booking

Bookings are made online; there is a single evening set menu, plus weekend lunch. Phone calls don't always get through during service hours, so use the website or email instead.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Only open for dinner Wednesday to Sunday, plus weekend lunch, and the room is small — book early; the phone is often unanswered, so the website or email works better.
  • The tasting menu is long and unhurried, so allow around three hours, and take a taxi straight to the second-floor entrance on Yifeng Road Section 4.
