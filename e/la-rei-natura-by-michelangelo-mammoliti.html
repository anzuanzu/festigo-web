🌿 La Rei Natura by Michelangelo Mammoliti

Serralunga d'Alba, Italy

Michelangelo Mammoliti · Creative, contemporary

A three-Michelin-star restaurant where the chef builds refined creative dishes around home-grown vegetables and Piedmontese terroir. The wine list is a Barolo deep dive, served by a young, polished team.

About €280–350 per person, drinks extra

Booking

Good to know

  • Open Wednesday to Sunday only, mostly dinner on weekdays and lunch on weekends; book early for peak slots.
  • It sits in the Langhe hills, so plan to drive or take a taxi from Alba and allow plenty of time for the long tasting menu.
