🏀 NBA Playoffs & Finals

Home arenas of qualifying teams, United States / Canada

Usually: Starts after the regular season ends in mid-April; the Finals are in JuneDates TBA

Sixteen teams battle over two months in best-of-seven series from the first round to the Finals—the atmosphere is on another level from the regular season.

Host cities depend on which teams advance, often only days ahead for each round. Book once matchups are set, and choose free-cancellation stays.

Good to know

  • First-round tickets are the easiest and cheapest to get.
  • Finals tickets often cost ten times regular-season prices.
