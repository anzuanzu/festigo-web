🎆 Penghu International Fireworks Festival

Magong, Penghu, Taiwan

Usually: Early May to late August, on fixed weeknights (2026: May 4 – Aug 25)Dates TBA

A summer-long fireworks season over the sea at Magong, with drone shows and about 30 nights of displays—pair it with island hopping.

Good to know

  • Flights and ferries to Penghu fill up fast—book early.
  • Shows run on fixed weeknights; check the official schedule before you go.
