🍽️ Cocina Hermanos Torres

Barcelona, Spain

Hermanos Torres (Sergio and Javier Torres) · Creative Mediterranean

A three-Michelin-star Barcelona restaurant where the Torres twins present creative Mediterranean cuisine around a theatrical open kitchen. Cured squid with poultry consommé and caviar is a signature, and five sommeliers oversee the cellar.

About €320 per person for the tasting menu, excluding drinks (wine pairing €195)

Booking
