🍷 Plénitude - Cheval Blanc Paris

Paris, France

Arnaud Donckele · Creative French

Plénitude inside Cheval Blanc Paris is a three-star French fine-dining experience where sauces take center stage. It is one of Paris’s hardest tables, so plan well ahead for a special occasion.

Tasting menus run several hundred euros per person; the Émotions menu is about €650, drinks extra

Booking

Online reservations open 60 days before the dining date at midnight CET; seats are extremely hard to get, so book right when they open and join the waitlist.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Reservations open 60 days before the dining date at midnight CET; popular slots fill fast, so book on time and join the waitlist.
  • The restaurant is by the Louvre and the Seine, a short walk from Louvre–Rivoli metro; the tasting menu takes time, so set aside the whole evening.
