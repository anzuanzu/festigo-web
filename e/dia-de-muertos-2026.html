💀 Día de Muertos

Mexico City, Mexico

Oct 31 – Nov 2, 2026

Marigolds, sugar skulls and ofrendas welcome back the dead; Mexico City stages a huge parade.

Good to know

  • Visit Xochimilco or Mixquic for traditional cemetery vigils.
  • The parade route is packed—watch your valuables.
