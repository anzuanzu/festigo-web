🍝 Osteria Francescana

Modena, Italy

Massimo Bottura · Creative, Contemporary Italian

Bottura's three-star flagship reinterprets Modenese tradition and Italian classics with playful precision. The sober dining room, hung with contemporary art, mirrors the creativity on the plate.

Tasting menu about €350 per person, drinks excluded

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings: request a table via the website or reserve@osteriafrancescana.it; the reservations office is reachable daily 10:00–19:00 (phone +39 059 223912).

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Tables are extremely hard to get — send your request as soon as bookings open and offer alternative dates; confirmation arrives by e-mail.
  • Both lunch (12:30–15:00) and dinner (20:00–22:30) are served, and lunch is usually easier to secure; the Viale Vittorio Veneto car park is a 2-minute walk away.
