🍽️ Schloss Schauenstein

Fürstenau, Switzerland

Andreas Caminada · Creative, Regional

Caminada's three-star flagship in a historic castle at the foot of Piz Beverin, building creative menus almost entirely on Graubünden produce and its own kitchen garden. The aperitif snacks alone amount to a menu before the menu.

Chef's Table CHF 650 per person including the exclusive menu and drinks pairing; main dining room menu prices not published

Booking

The house handles its own bookings via the website form or by phone on +41 81 632 10 80; both hotel and restaurant take reservations up to eight months ahead.

Good to know

  • Bookings open at most eight months ahead and peak dates go fast — use the website form or call +41 81 632 10 80 right when the window opens.
