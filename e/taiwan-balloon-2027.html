🎈 Taiwan International Balloon Festival

Luye, Taitung, Taiwan

Usually: Early July to late August (2026: Jul 4 – Aug 20)Dates TBA

All summer, dozens of themed hot-air balloons rise over Luye Highland at dawn and dusk, with glow shows and tethered rides.

Good to know

  • Book tethered rides online in advance—they sell out in seconds.
  • Flights are only at dawn and dusk, not on Tuesdays, and are cancelled in strong wind.
