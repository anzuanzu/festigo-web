🏃 London Marathon

London, United Kingdom

Apr 25, 2027

One of the seven Majors, from Greenwich past Tower Bridge and Canary Wharf to The Mall—famous for costumed runners and charity fundraising.

Good to know

  • Entry is by ballot (opens the April before, over a million applicants) or charity places. Spectating is free.
  • Tower Bridge is the highlight for runners and the most crowded viewpoint—arrive early.
