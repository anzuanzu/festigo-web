🍗 Daegu Chimaek Festival

Daegu, South Korea

Usually: About five days in early JulyDates TBA

Celebrating Korea's beloved chicken-and-beer (chimaek) culture in its birthplace, with major fried chicken brands and craft beers at Duryu Park.

Good to know

  • Evenings bring live concerts and the liveliest crowds.
  • Daegu is scorching in July—stay hydrated during the day.
