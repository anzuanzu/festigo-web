👑 Grand Palace Bangkok

Bangkok, Thailand

Best time: November to February, the cool and dry season

The royal palace complex, packed with gilded stupas, mosaic-covered halls and guardian giants. Inside, Wat Phra Kaew houses the Emerald Buddha, Thailand's most sacred image.

Good to know

  • Shoulders and knees must be covered: no shorts, sleeveless tops or leggings.
  • Arrive at the 8:30 opening to beat the midday heat and tour groups.
