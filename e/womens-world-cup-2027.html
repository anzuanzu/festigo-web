⚽️ FIFA Women's World Cup

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Jun 24 – Jul 25, 2027

The first Women's World Cup in South America, with 32 teams across eight host cities. Opening and final venues are still to be announced; the Maracanã is a favourite.

Good to know

  • Group matches are spread across Brazil—fly between host cities.
  • Copacabana and Ipanema are the most convenient bases in Rio.
