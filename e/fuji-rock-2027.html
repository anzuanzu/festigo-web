🎸 Fuji Rock Festival

Naeba, Niigata, Japan

Jul 23 – 25, 2027

Japan's largest outdoor music festival, set in the forested Naeba ski resort—famous as the world's cleanest festival.

Good to know

  • Mountain weather changes fast—pack rain gear and boots.
  • Naeba Prince rooms are by lottery; most stay in Echigo-Yuzawa.
