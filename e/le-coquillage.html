🦞 Le Coquillage

Saint-Méloir-des-Ondes, France

Hugo Roellinger · Creative, seafood

Hugo Roellinger serves refined Breton seafood at a romantic manor overlooking Mont-Saint-Michel Bay, using herbs and seaweed from his own gardens. The three-Michelin-star experience is a memorable choice for a special dinner.

About €215–275 per person for set menus
