🏗️ Sagrada Família

Barcelona, Spain

Best time: May–June and September–October; morning light lights up the stained glass

Gaudí's life's work, under construction since 1882. Its facades look like melting stone, its columns rise like a forest, and stained glass floods the nave with rainbow light.

Good to know

  • Tickets are online only and often sell out two or three weeks ahead in peak season.
  • Add a tower visit to see the spires up close and the city beyond.
