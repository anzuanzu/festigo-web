🥨 Sühring

Bangkok, Thailand

Mathias & Thomas Sühring (twin chefs) · Modern German, creative

Twin chefs Mathias and Thomas Sühring translate childhood memories and family recipes into a refined modern German tasting menu. Set in a restored 1970s villa, it offers an elegant, intimate setting for a special occasion.

From about €258 per person, drinks extra

Booking

Bookings are made by phone or email; for parties over 8, call +66 2107 2777 or email directly.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Lunch is served Thursday to Sunday and dinner Wednesday to Sunday; closed Monday and Tuesday.
  • The restaurant is in a residential villa area, so a taxi or ride-hailing app is easiest; parties over 8 must contact the restaurant directly.
