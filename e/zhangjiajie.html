⛰️ Zhangjiajie

Zhangjiajie, China

Best time: April–May and September–October; mist often follows rain

Thousands of sandstone pillars shoot up from the forest, appearing to float when wrapped in mist: the inspiration for the floating mountains of Avatar.

Good to know

  • Tickets are valid for four days; allow at least two for Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain.
  • Tianmen Mountain's glass skywalk and 999 steps are across town and need another day.
