🐉 Ha Long Bay

Ha Long, Vietnam

Best time: March–May and September–November, when the weather is most settled

Nearly 2,000 limestone islands rising from emerald water, said to be jewels spat out by a dragon sent to protect Vietnam. An overnight cruise and kayaking through the grottoes are the highlights.

Good to know

  • Choose an overnight cruise to reach quieter Lan Ha Bay.
  • Cruises can be cancelled in typhoon season (July–September), so keep plans flexible.
