🏮 Pingxi Sky Lantern Festival

Pingxi, New Taipei, Taiwan

Feb 20, 2027

On Lantern Festival night, thousands of sky lanterns rise at once—often listed among the world's must-see festivals.

Good to know

  • The Pingxi rail line is jammed; use the shuttle buses.
  • Stay in Taipei and do it as a day trip.
