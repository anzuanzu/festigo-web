🍷 Hospices de Beaune Wine Auction

Beaune, France

Nov 13 – 15, 2026

Burgundy's most important charity wine auction turns the town into a weekend-long wine festival with open cellars.

Good to know

  • Auction seats are limited, but street tastings are open to all.
  • Beaune has few hotels—stay in Dijon and take the train.
