🏃 Sydney Marathon

Sydney, Australia

Aug 29, 2027

The newest World Marathon Major since 2025: start in North Sydney, cross the Harbour Bridge and finish at the Opera House.

Good to know

  • Entry is by ballot (September–October the year before); you only pay if selected. Spectating is free.
  • Late August is late winter there—early starts are cold, so bring a jacket.
