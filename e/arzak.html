🦞 Arzak

San Sebastián, Spain

Juan Mari Arzak and his daughter Elena Arzak · Traditional Basque, Contemporary

A century-old family mansion in San Sebastián's Alto de Miracruz, Michelin-starred since 1974 and a temple of Basque cooking. The father-daughter team blends laboratory-driven innovation with deep tradition, backed by a wine list of over 3,000 labels.

TripAdvisor lists a price range of about R2,641–3,018 per person

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings; the only official channel is www.arzak.es. It stresses that it never asks for advance payment, so avoid look-alike sites found via search engines.
