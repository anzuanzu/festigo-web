🌙 Ramadan & Eid in Istanbul

Istanbul, Türkiye

Feb 8 – Mar 11, 2027

After sunset during Ramadan, the squares by the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia fill with iftar meals, night markets, mosque lights and performances, ending in three days of Eid.

Good to know

  • Avoid eating or drinking in public during the day out of respect for those fasting.
  • Many Turks travel over the Eid holiday—transport and hotels fill up.
