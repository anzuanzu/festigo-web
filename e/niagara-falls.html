🌈 Niagara Falls

Niagara Falls, Canada

Best time: June to September when all boat tours run; in winter the falls partly freeze

The great falls on the US–Canada border, where thousands of tonnes of water a second pour over Horseshoe Falls. A boat ride into the spray at their base is unforgettable.

Good to know

  • The Canadian side has the best view of Horseshoe Falls.
  • In summer the falls are lit nightly, with fireworks on many evenings.
