🌸 Jinhae Gunhangje Festival

Changwon, South Korea

Usually: About 10 days from late March to early AprilDates TBA

Korea's largest cherry blossom festival, with over 300,000 trees around a naval port town; Yeojwacheon's bridge is a K-drama icon.

Good to know

  • Easiest as a day trip from Busan.
  • Weekend traffic is brutal—go on a weekday.
