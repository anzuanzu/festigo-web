🍷 La Pergola

Rome, Italy

Heinz Beck · Mediterranean, Contemporary

A three-Michelin-star dining room on Monte Mario, where Heinz Beck applies a light, precise touch to Mediterranean and contemporary cooking. The terrace view over Rome and the strong wine program make it a special-occasion destination.

About €210–245 per person, drinks extra

Jacket required for gentlemen; tie optional. No specific requirement for ladies, but the restaurant is formal.

Booking

Reservations are accepted up to one year ahead; the restaurant suggests booking about 2 months ahead for Tue–Thu and 3–4 months for Fri–Sat. The reservations office is open Mon–Sat, 9:30am–6:30pm CET.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Book well ahead: about 2 months for Tue–Thu and 3–4 months for Fri–Sat; reservations can be made up to one year in advance.
  • Dinner only, and gentlemen should bring a jacket; it is inside the Rome Cavalieri hotel, so a taxi is the easiest way to arrive.
