🎆 Hong Kong New Year Countdown

Hong Kong, Hong Kong SAR

Dec 31, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027

Skyscrapers on both sides of Victoria Harbour count down with lights and fireworks—one of Asia's signature New Year celebrations.

Good to know

  • Fireworks were cancelled for 2025 after the Tai Po fire and replaced by a light show in Central—check the Tourism Board for this year's format.
  • The Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront is the classic spot; crowd control starts by early evening.
