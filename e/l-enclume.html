🌿 L'Enclume

Cartmel, United Kingdom

Simon Rogan · Creative British, farm-to-table

Daily-picked produce from Simon Rogan's own 12-acre farm drives a precise, inventive British tasting menu. The signature 'Anvil' dessert shows deep technique, served with relaxed polish in a tiny Lakeland village.

About £125–275 per person, drinks and 10% service extra (Lunch £125, L'Enclume Experience £275)

No dress code; wear whatever feels most comfortable

Booking

All reservations are released on a first-come, first-served basis and require credit card details to secure the table; signing up to the newsletter brings priority booking invitations.
