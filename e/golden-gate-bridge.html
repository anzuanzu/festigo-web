🌁 Golden Gate Bridge

San Francisco, United States

Best time: September and October, when the fog is lightest

The International Orange suspension bridge across the Golden Gate strait, often half-hidden in sea fog, is the West Coast's most famous sight. You can walk or cycle across.

Good to know

  • Battery Spencer at the north end gives the classic view of the whole bridge.
  • Rent a bike, ride across to Sausalito and take the ferry back.
