🍷 Casa Perbellini 12 Apostoli

Verona, Italy

Giancarlo Perbellini · Creative, Contemporary

A historic Verona restaurant where Giancarlo Perbellini serves contemporary tasting menus. The Chef’s Table has just 12 seats and lets guests watch the kitchen brigade at work.

Chef’s Table €395 per person, wines included; other menu prices not listed.

Elegant or smart casual is recommended; shorts and flip-flops are discouraged.
