🏃 New York City Marathon

New York, United States

Nov 1, 2026

One of the seven World Marathon Majors and the world's largest marathon, running through all five boroughs to Central Park.

Good to know

  • Entry is by drawing (applications in February), qualifying time or charity. Spectating is free.
  • First Avenue has the loudest crowds.
