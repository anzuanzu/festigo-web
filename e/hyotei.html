★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026

🍱 Hyotei

Kyoto, Japan

Yoshihiro Takahashi · Kaiseki (tea kaiseki)

A 400-year-old tea house turned ryotei at the gate of Nanzen-ji, known for tea kaiseki, its signature egg and summer morning porridge. Seasonal gardens and quiet tea rooms set a serene pace.

About ¥40,000–53,000 per person, tax and service included, drinks extra

Smart casual; please avoid sandals

Booking

Bookings are mainly by phone and open roughly four months ahead; breakfast seatings can only be reserved through your hotel's concierge.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • The morning porridge (summer) and quail porridge (winter) are served in the annex at a far lower price than the main-store kaiseki, but must be booked via a hotel concierge.
  • The main store closes on Wednesdays and last dinner orders are at 19:00; it is a 6–10 minute walk from Keage Station on the Tozai subway line, with parking available.
