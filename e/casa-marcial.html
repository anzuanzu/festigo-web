🍽️ Casa Marcial

Arriondas, Spain

Nacho Manzano · Innovative Asturian cuisine

Casa Marcial is Nacho Manzano’s deeply personal showcase of Asturian produce and memory, presented with contemporary precision. Its remote mountain setting makes the three-star meal feel like a destination pilgrimage.

About €205–270 per person depending on the tasting menu, drinks extra

No sandals or shorts; confirming a reservation means agreeing to the dress code.

Booking
