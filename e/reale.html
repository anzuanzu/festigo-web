🍷 Reale

Castel di Sangro, Italy

Niko Romito · Modern, creative Italian

In a 16th-century monastery, Niko Romito uses rigorous minimalism to draw out Abruzzo ingredients with startling clarity of flavour. The bread and bakery work is so refined it nearly counts as a course of its own.

Tasting menu from about €250 (US$272) per person, drinks extra
