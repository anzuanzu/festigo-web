★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026

🍷 Maison Lameloise

Chagny, France

Éric Pras · Modern French

A Burgundy institution since 1926, where Éric Pras reinterprets regional produce such as snails and Bresse poultry with a modern touch. The wine list, especially by the glass, is a highlight.

About €200 per person on average, drinks extra

Smart and flexible: men are required to wear a formal jacket, trousers and a collared shirt (jackets relaxed in summer); ladies are invited to dress accordingly.

Booking

The restaurant and hotel are closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and shut from December 21 to January 21 each year; book directly through the official reservation page, ideally several weeks ahead.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays, plus a long annual break from late December to late January — check dates before booking.
  • Walkable from Chagny station (hourly trains on the Dijon–Lyon line); by car take A6 exit 25.1 from Paris or 25.2 from Lyon.
