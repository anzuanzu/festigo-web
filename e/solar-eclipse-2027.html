🌑 Total Solar Eclipse 2027 (Luxor)

Luxor, Egypt

Aug 2, 2027

The longest total solar eclipse visible from land this century: about 6 minutes 20 seconds of totality over Luxor's temples. Nothing this long returns until 2114.

Good to know

  • Luxor hotels are already booking up a year ahead—reserve as early as you can.
  • Use ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses; remove them only during totality.
