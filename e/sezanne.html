🥖 Sézanne

Tokyo, Japan

Stephen Lancaster · French

A modern French restaurant inside Four Seasons Hotel Tokyo at Marunouchi, using Japanese seasonal produce with a light, precise touch. Its three-star experience pairs inventive sauces with exceptionally attentive service.

About ¥56,000–88,000 per person plus 15% service charge; actual spend often reaches ¥100,000 or more, drinks extra

Smart casual; men should wear a jacket or a collared long-sleeved shirt. No short-sleeved shirts, T-shirts, shorts, tank tops or sandals.

Booking

Bookings can be made via Omakase or by phone; no fixed booking-opening time is stated, so plan early.
