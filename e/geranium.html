🌿 Geranium

Copenhagen, Denmark

Rasmus Kofoed · Creative, contemporary Nordic; meat-free, built on vegetables and seafood

Set on the eighth floor of Copenhagen's Parken Stadium, Rasmus Kofoed's kitchen builds some fifteen precise, artful courses from vegetables, foraged plants and sustainable seafood. Panoramic park and city views give the bright dining room a calm, airy feel.

About DKK 4,400–4,500 per person, drinks and pairings extra
