👗 Tokyo Girls Collection

Shibuya, Tokyo, Japan

Usually: A Saturday in early-to-mid March (spring/summer show)Dates TBA

Japan's biggest public fashion event—anyone can buy a ticket to see runway shows, live performances and brand pop-ups.

Good to know

  • Tickets come in general and S seats; S seats are closest to the runway.
  • The show runs from midday into the evening, with re-entry allowed.
