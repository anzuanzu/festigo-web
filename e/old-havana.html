🚗 Old Havana

Havana, Cuba

Best time: November to April, the drier and cooler season

The Spanish colonial heart of Havana: pastel baroque buildings, 1950s classic cars in the streets and salsa drifting from every doorway, as if time stopped half a century ago.

Good to know

  • Cuba requires a tourist card (visa) arranged before departure.
  • US-issued cards don't work in Cuba, so bring enough cash.
