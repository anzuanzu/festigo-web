Quique Dacosta · Creative cuisine

Quique Dacosta is known for an avant-garde tasting menu that changes almost yearly, turning Mediterranean flavours into a multisensory artistic experience. This small seaside restaurant in Dénia is a benchmark for creative Spanish cuisine.
