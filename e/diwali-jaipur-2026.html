🪔 Diwali

Jaipur, India

Nov 8, 2026

India's biggest festival of lights, with oil lamps and fireworks everywhere. Jaipur's bazaars glow with spectacular decorations.

Good to know

  • Markets are liveliest the days before; many shops close on the day itself.
  • Fireworks run late—stay outside the old city if you're noise-sensitive.
