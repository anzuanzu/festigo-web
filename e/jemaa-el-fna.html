🫖 Jemaa el-Fnaa

Marrakesh, Morocco

Best time: March–May and September–November, avoiding the summer heat

The beating heart of Marrakesh's medina: juice stalls and snake charmers by day, a smoky open-air food court by night, ringed by labyrinthine souks.

Good to know

  • Photographing performers or animals means paying; if you won't pay, don't shoot.
  • At dusk, head to a café rooftop to watch the square light up.
