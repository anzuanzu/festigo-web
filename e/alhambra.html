🌹 Alhambra

Granada, Spain

Best time: March–May and September–November, when the gardens bloom and the heat is bearable

The last great palace of Moorish Spain. The Nasrid Palaces' honeycomb ceilings and carved Arabic script are breathtaking, and at sunset the whole fortress glows beneath the snowy Sierra Nevada.

Good to know

  • Daily numbers are capped: book a month or more ahead in peak season and arrive on time for your Nasrid Palaces slot.
  • At sunset, watch the Alhambra glow from the Mirador de San Nicolás.
