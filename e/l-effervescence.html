🥖 L'Effervescence

Tokyo, Japan

Shinobu Namae · French (innovative French)

A three-Michelin-star French restaurant in Nishiazabu where Shinobu Namae marries Japanese ingredients with classic French technique. The tasting menu lasts three to four hours and features his signature apple pie and long slow-cooked turnip.

About ¥24,000–57,000 per person, drinks extra

No casual clothing such as shorts, sweatsuits, hoodies or sandals; hats and outerwear (including puffer vests) are not allowed in the dining room. Gentlemen need a jacket or collared shirt, and perfume or cologne should be avoided.

Booking

Bookings can be made via the official website, online platforms or by phone; the restaurant reconfirms one week ahead and will cancel the booking if it is not confirmed three days before.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • With only around 40-some seats, competition for reservations is intense, so plan well ahead; cancellations by parties of 2–4 within three days incur 50% of the course price, and same-day cancellations or no-shows 100%.
  • Lunch is less expensive than dinner and easier to secure; the full experience takes about three to four hours, so allow plenty of time.
