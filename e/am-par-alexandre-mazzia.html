🍷 AM par Alexandre Mazzia

Marseille, France

Alexandre Mazzia · Creative cuisine (French technique with spices, smoke and Congolese influences)

A three-star address near Marseille's Stade Vélodrome, where some forty tiny, precisely built plates carry spices, smoke and the chef's Congolese childhood memories. A house collection of 400-plus vinegars gives his jus depth and tension.

About €400–500 per person, drinks extra

Booking

Tables are booked online through TheFork; the restaurant only opens Wednesday to Saturday, with both a lunch and a dinner service.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Open only Wednesday to Saturday with very few seats, so book online as soon as your dates are fixed.
  • The tasting menu runs to dozens of courses, so allow three to four hours; dinner is the more relaxed option.
