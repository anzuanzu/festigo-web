🔥 Up Helly Aa

Lerwick, United Kingdom

Jan 26, 2027

Shetland's Viking fire festival: around a thousand torch-bearers march through the winter night and set a hand-built Viking galley ablaze—Europe's largest fire festival.

Good to know

  • The torch procession is free to watch; the night-long hall parties need tickets or invitations.
  • Reach Shetland by domestic flight or overnight ferry from Edinburgh or Aberdeen.
