💃 Awa Odori

Tokushima, Japan

Aug 12 – 15, 2027

Japan's largest dance festival, with 100,000+ dancers over four nights—'the dancers are fools, the watchers are fools, so why not dance?'

Good to know

  • Paid viewing stands have the best view and can be booked ahead.
  • Tokushima lodging is scarce; many stay in Osaka or Takamatsu.
