🎆 Paris New Year's Eve on the Champs-Élysées

Paris, France

Dec 31, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027

The Arc de Triomphe becomes a giant projection screen, then fireworks burst over the Champs-Élysées for around a million revellers.

Good to know

  • There's a security perimeter—no glass or fireworks, so allow time for checks.
  • Nearby metro stations close for the night; walk to one further away afterwards.
