🦀 Sapporo Autumn Fest

Sapporo, Japan

Sep 10 – Oct 2, 2027

Hokkaido's biggest food festival fills Odori Park with king crab, sea urchin, Genghis Khan lamb and local craft beer.

Good to know

  • Weekday lunchtimes have the shortest queues.
  • September is also autumn salmon and new-rice season.
