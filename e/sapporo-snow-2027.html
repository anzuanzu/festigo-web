⛄️ Sapporo Snow Festival

Sapporo, Japan

Feb 4 – 11, 2027

Giant snow and ice sculptures fill Odori Park, glowing after dark. Hokkaido's biggest winter event.

Good to know

  • Sapporo hotel prices often double during the festival—book early.
  • Bring shoe grips; sidewalks get icy.
