🎇 Nagaoka Fireworks Festival

Nagaoka, Niigata, Japan

Aug 2 – 3, 2027

One of Japan's three great fireworks shows, held every August 2–3 on the Shinano River, famous for the two-kilometre-wide 'Phoenix' display.

Good to know

  • Viewing seats are sold or allocated by lottery in advance; free areas are limited.
  • Nagaoka hotels fill a year ahead—stay in Niigata City or take the shinkansen from Tokyo.
