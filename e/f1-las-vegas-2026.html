🏎️ Las Vegas Grand Prix

Las Vegas, United States

Nov 19 – 21, 2026

A night street race on the Las Vegas Strip, with cars topping 300 km/h past neon casinos.

Good to know

  • Racing runs late at night and November desert nights are cold—bring a jacket.
  • Strip hotels spike on race weekend; consider Downtown.
