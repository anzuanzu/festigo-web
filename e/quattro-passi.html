🦑 Quattro Passi

Nerano, Italy

Fabrizio Mellino · Mediterranean, Contemporary

A three-generation family house above the bay at Nerano, where Fabrizio Mellino cooks Campanian seafood with light, precise contemporary technique — witness the squid flower with scampi tartare and caviar. It is hard to reach and hard to book, and worth the trip.

About €300–500 per person, drinks extra

Smart casual

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings, so you contact it directly; for summer weekends plan two to three months ahead, or more.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Tables are genuinely hard to get: aim for two to three months out, and earlier for summer weekends on the Amalfi Coast.
  • The drive from Sorrento is roughly 18 km of narrow coastal road, so a taxi or private transfer is easiest; a lunch booking avoids the late return trip.
