🥖 Robuchon au Dôme

Macau, Macau SAR

Julien Tongourian · Contemporary French

Perched on the 43rd floor of Grand Lisboa, this Michelin three-star restaurant serves refined contemporary French cuisine with sweeping Macau views. Its dessert trolley and deep wine list make the experience especially memorable.

About MOP 1,100–2,200 per person, drinks extra

Formal attire; shorts and flip-flops are not allowed

Booking

Book directly via the official website; seats open about three months ahead. A MOP 500 per-person deposit is required and deducted from the bill.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Booking is competitive; reserve about three months ahead via the official website, and lunch is better value than dinner.
  • Formal attire is required; take a taxi to Grand Lisboa and request a window table when booking.
