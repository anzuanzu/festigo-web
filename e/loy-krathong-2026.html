🏮 Loy Krathong & Yi Peng

Chiang Mai, Thailand

Nov 24 – 26, 2026

On the full moon of the twelfth Thai month, people float krathongs on rivers and release lanterns into the sky. Chiang Mai's Yi Peng is a bucket-list sight for photographers.

Good to know

  • Official lantern-release events require tickets that sell out quickly.
  • Old City and Ping River hotels book up first.
