🎨 Holi

Mathura, India

Mar 22, 2027

The festival of colours welcoming spring. Mathura and Vrindavan, Krishna's legendary home, host the biggest celebrations.

Good to know

  • Wear old white clothes and oil your skin and hair beforehand.
  • Crowds are intense—solo women should join a group.
