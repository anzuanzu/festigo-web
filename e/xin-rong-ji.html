🦀 Xin Rong Ji

Chengdu, China

Ma Lin · Taizhou cuisine

This Chengdu branch of Xin Rong Ji serves refined Taizhou cuisine in a spacious dining room with Twin Towers views. Premium seasonal ingredients and the amber wine-marinated crab are highlights.

Approx. RMB 1,200–1,500 per person
