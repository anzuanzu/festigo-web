🦜 Iguazú Falls

Puerto Iguazú, Argentina

Best time: March–May and August–November, with strong flow and milder heat

Some 275 cascades stretching nearly three kilometres along the Argentina–Brazil border. At the end of the Devil's Throat walkway, walls of roaring water surround you on three sides.

Good to know

  • The Argentine side gets you close; the Brazilian side shows the panorama. Do both.
  • Bring a rain jacket and a dry bag; you will get soaked near the falls.
