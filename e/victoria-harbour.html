⛴️ Victoria Harbour

Hong Kong, Hong Kong SAR

Best time: October to December, when the air is dry, cool and clear

The deep natural harbour between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon, ringed by towers rising against the hills. Seen from the Tsim Sha Tsui promenade or the Peak, it is one of the world's great skylines.

Good to know

  • The Star Ferry costs a few Hong Kong dollars and is the best-value way to see the harbour.
  • The Symphony of Lights show runs at 8 pm nightly; watch it from the Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront.
