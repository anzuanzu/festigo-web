🌸 Tokyo Cherry Blossom Season

Tokyo, Japan

Usually: Late March to early April, depending on the bloom forecastDates TBA

From late March to early April, Ueno Park, Meguro River and Chidorigafuchi burst into bloom, with magical night illuminations.

Good to know

  • Bloom dates shift yearly—check the sakura forecast two weeks out.
  • Shinjuku Gyoen bans alcohol, making it a calmer spot.
