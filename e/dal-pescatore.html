🍷 Dal Pescatore

Runate, Italy

Nadia Santini · Italian Contemporary, Classic Cuisine

A legendary family-run Italian restaurant that has held three Michelin stars since 1996. It is known for classic Italian cooking, estate-grown ingredients and refined desserts.

Tasting menus from about €225 (Menu d'Estate) to €310 (Menu del Pescatore) per person, service and taxes included

Booking

The restaurant handles its own bookings; contact it directly by phone at +39 0376 723001 or by email at santini@dalpescatore.com.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • Bookings are handled by the restaurant itself, so call or email directly. It is closed Monday and Tuesday, serves dinner Wednesday to Sunday, and also offers lunch Friday to Sunday.
  • The restaurant is in the countryside between Cremona and Mantua, so driving is recommended. Consider the Friday-to-Sunday lunch service as an option.
