★★★ Three Michelin stars, 2026

🌿 Miyamaso

Kyoto, Japan

Hisato Nakahigashi · Japanese cuisine (kaiseki, wild herb cuisine)

Once a pilgrims' lodging for Bujoji Temple, this dining inn now sits deep in the Hanase valley. Wild herbs, river fish and game carry the seasons, rustic yet precise.

About ¥22,770–¥33,880 per person (tax and service included, approx. US$140–210); a mid-tier menu at ¥27,830 is also offered; drinks not included

Booking

The inn handles its own bookings, so no booking platform can take your table; call the ryokan directly. English may be limited, so ask early and consider having your hotel help.

FestiGo is not affiliated with Michelin. Stars, prices and booking details are compiled from public sources; Michelin and the restaurants have the final word.

Good to know

  • It sits in the mountains where buses are infrequent and stop early, so drive or have the inn arrange a taxi; allow about an hour from central Kyoto.
  • It is also a ryokan, so staying overnight lets you dine unhurried — the early-morning mountain scenery is the reward.
