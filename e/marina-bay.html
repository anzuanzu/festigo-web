🌳 Marina Bay

Singapore, Singapore

Best time: Year-round; February to April is drier

Singapore's showpiece: the ship-topped Marina Bay Sands, the towering Supertrees and the world's largest glass greenhouses. After dark, the Supertree light show and the bay's water show take turns.

Good to know

  • The free Garden Rhapsody light show runs at 7:45 and 8:45 pm.
  • The Cloud Forest and Flower Dome need tickets but are perfect on rainy days.
